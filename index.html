Dear Mom,

Happy Birthday! I have been on quite the streak of making websites over birthday cards. I find them much more personalized because instead of just decorating the front of a card, you can decorate an entire website. I also like the permancence that you get with a website!

I really want you to know how grateful I am for everything you've been doing for me. Thank you so much for helping me get everything set up for my new job, and for helping me navigate the apartment search. I do not know how I could have gotten through everything without your help and guidance.

I cannot wait to look at apartments with you this weekend! I am so glad that you also look forward to that. Your feedback means so much to me, and I value your opinion more than anyone else. I always feel better about decisions when I've had a chance to talk them through with you.

I'm also really looking forward to browsing Craigslist for furniture with you! I know it might sound small, but getting to do these things together and having your input makes everything so much more enjoyable.

And of course, I love our time watching Brothers & Sisters together. The time flies so fast, and you are always able to fill me in if I miss parts of an episode. It's these little moments that I appreciate more than I let on.

I also want to thank you for everything with the Lily situation. You made everything so much better, not just for me, but for her as well. The way you listened, gave your input, and asked me the right questions meant more to me than I can explain. Having you there to help me think through everything made such a difference.

I hope you realize how much of an impact you have on my life. Whether it's the big things or the little everyday things, you are always there, and I never take that for granted.

Thank you for being someone I can always turn to, someone whose opinion I trust, and someone who makes everything feel a little easier and a lot better.

I hope this next year brings you as much happiness as you've brought to the people around you. You deserve every bit of it.

I love you, and I'm so lucky to have you.

Happy Birthday, with all my love, Nathaniel ♡

One more thing...

Here's to you, Mom.

To more apartment hunting, furniture adventures, Brothers & Sisters episodes, and all the little moments that make life special.

I hope your birthday is as wonderful as you are. Thank you for everything. I love you so much! ♡
